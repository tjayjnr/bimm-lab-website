    Biography

    Dr. Ashok Kumar Ghosh is an Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering at New Mexico Tech, where he has taught and led research since 2003. He earned his B.Tech with Honors in Civil Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Kharagpur (1983), an M.S. in Structural Engineering from Washington State University (1985), and a Ph.D. in Engineering from IIT Kharagpur (1993). A licensed Professional Engineer in the State of New Mexico, his research centers on the mechanics of fluid-filled multifunctional composite materials, with applications in radiation shielding, space habitat protection, and biomimetic heat pipe and radiator design for spaceflight. His work has been supported by NASA’s New Mexico Space Grant Consortium, the National Science Foundation, the Office of Naval Research, the Department of Energy, and other agencies, totaling more than $3 million across 18 funded projects. He has authored more than 70 peer-reviewed journal and conference publications and holds five patents in the U.S. and India. Dr. Ghosh is also President of Composite Solution LLC and has served as a Fulbright Specialist, a DOE Visiting Faculty member at Los Alamos National Laboratory, an AFOSR Summer Faculty researcher at the Air Force Research Laboratory, and President of the New Mexico Solar Energy Association.

    Research Interests

    • Mechanics of fluid-filled multifunctional composites and their applications in engineering
    • Radiation shielding materials
    • Biomimetic design for space habitats and spaceflight systems
    • Laminated composite structures
