    Sameer Jain

    Assistant Lab Manager, M.S. Mechanical Engineering — New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology

    Sameer Jain is an M.S. Mechanical Engineering student at New Mexico Tech, advised by Dr. Ashok Ghosh. His background is in 3D printing, topology optimization, and CAD modeling, with years of hands-on experience in SolidWorks and Autodesk Inventor. His current thesis research focuses on designing and building an in-house reduced-gravity drop tower facility to study capillary-driven fluid transport through porous, bio-inspired composite structures for space habitat applications, as part of NASA’s AQUASHIELD program.

    He is a co-author on two peer-reviewed publications — one on turmeric-coated antibacterial concrete (MRS Communications, 2024) and another on doped hydroxyapatite composites for orthopedic and dental applications (Journal of Materials Research, 2025). He has presented his research at the Student Research Symposium and the Rio Grande Symposium on Advanced Materials. He is also comfortable with Overleaf for technical writing and has some experience in Adobe Illustrator.

    Outside of research, he serves as President of Hindu YUVA at New Mexico Tech and as Sponsor Liaison for the Junior Design 3D-Printed Aircraft Competition Team.
