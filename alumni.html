Alumni

Former BIMM Lab members who have completed their graduate degrees, listed with their thesis or dissertation work.

Gabriel D. Maestas — Ph.D., Mechanical Engineering (Intelligent Energetic Systems) · Defended April 2026

“Flexural Characterization of Fluid-Filled, Sandwich Core Composites: Development of Experimental Methods, Plate Theory, and Finite Element Validation.”

Developed the Biaxial Clamped Plate Flexure Test, a new test method for fluid-filled sandwich composites inspired by human cortical bone, validated with FEA and digital image correlation. This work was recognized by the AIAA Materials Technical Committee in Aerospace America’s 2025 Year-in-Review as a paradigm shift in composite characterization methodology.

Nicholas Allen Hutt — M.S., Civil Engineering · Defended August 2025

“Investigation of Fluid-Filled Cellular Core Materials: Mechanical Properties Under Compression and Its Numerical Validation.”

Characterized how fluid saturation affects the mechanical and permeability behavior of open-cell foams, combining compression testing, Darcy permeability testing, SEM imaging, and ANSYS finite element analysis.

Owen Telford — M.S., Mechanical Engineering · Defended July 2023

“Investigation of a Biomimetic Heat Pipe Design for Improved Heat Transfer in Space Radiators.”

Fabricated and tested a biomimetic heat pipe against a conventional design for space radiator applications, validated with hot-oil-bath thermal testing.
