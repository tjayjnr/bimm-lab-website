BIMM Lab

Bio-Inspired Multifunctional Materials Laboratory

We develop fluid-filled composite materials inspired by biological structures — engineering the way nature layers, channels, and circulates fluids within tissue into materials with structural, thermal, and radiation-shielding function built in from the start.

What we work on

Our research centers on the mechanics of fluid-filled multifunctional composites: panels and structures that carry mechanical load while also shielding against radiation, managing heat, or performing other secondary functions — much as biological structures accomplish several jobs at once with a single tissue system. Current applications include radiation shielding, protective structures for space habitats, and biomimetic heat-transfer components for spaceflight.
