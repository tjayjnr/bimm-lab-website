    Patrick Pillers

    Undergraduate Research Assistant, B.S. Mechanical Engineering — New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology

    Patrick Pillers is a sophomore Mechanical Engineering student who currently serves as a Laboratory Assistant for the EPSCoR NASA AQUASHIELD project within the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology. In this capacity, he conducts fluid dynamics data analysis in the investigation of capillary rise phenomena across varying physical test conditions.

    His primary responsibilities include processing frame-by-frame video footage to accurately evaluate interfacial fluid dynamics and liquid height progression over time. Utilizing these measurements, Patrick develops graphical models that define individual properties that allow for further optimization of the overall product design of the fluid management system.
